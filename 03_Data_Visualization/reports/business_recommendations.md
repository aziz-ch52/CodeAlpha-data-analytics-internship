# Strategic Business Recommendations

Based on the exploratory data analysis and visual diagnostics of the 50,000 global sales records, we recommend the following strategic actions:

## 1. Optimize the Discount Strategy
* **Context:** The correlation heatmap shows that `discount_percent` has a negative correlation with `total_revenue` (**r = -0.14**). 
* **Action:** Immediately audit the discounting strategy. We are actively eroding profit margins without driving the expected proportional increase in sales volume. A/B test lower discount tiers in the upcoming quarter.

## 2. Capitalize on High-Variance Categories
* **Context:** The boxplot analysis revealed extreme high-value transactions within the **Home & Kitchen** category.
* **Action:** Develop a VIP or B2B sales pipeline specifically targeting buyers in this category. These outliers likely represent enterprise or bulk buyers whose needs differ fundamentally from standard retail consumers.

## 3. Inventory Alignment with Seasonality
* **Context:** The monthly trend analysis identified a distinct revenue peak in **January**.
* **Action:** Supply chain and procurement teams must front-load inventory for the **Beauty** and **Home & Kitchen** categories at least 45 days prior to January to prevent stockouts, which historically bottleneck total potential revenue.
